01 How to use Route2Zero+
  1. Choose a city or keep the Metro Manila view.
  2. Adjust the four weights to reflect a policy scenario.
  3. Select a route or click a priority point on the map.
  4. Review its street-following corridor, score drivers and known limitations.
  5. Export the current evidence pack as PDF, Word or CSV.
02 Why this matters+

Electrification resources are limited, while Metro Manila has a large and diverse jeepney network. Route2Zero creates a transparent first-pass shortlist so city teams can focus expensive field validation, grid studies and cooperative engagement where potential public value is strongest.

03 Method, assumptions and limitations+

Emissions potentialRoute kilometres × estimated weekday trips. This is an activity proxy, not measured tailpipe emissions.

EquityPopulation-weighted exposure to high-density 2020 WorldPop cells within a 300 m corridor catchment.

Grid feasibility2024 Luzon renewable generation share. It is not local charger or substation capacity.

Operator readinessNeutral 50/100 placeholder until cooperative financing workshops provide route-level evidence.

GeometryMapbox builds a drivable street path for the selected route from ordered GTFS coordinates. It is a planning visualisation, not an official franchise boundary.

AI boundaryOptional AI writes explanations after deterministic scoring. Model output never changes scores or ranks.

04 Recommended next-step pilot+

Validate the highest-priority corridors with LGUs and cooperatives; collect fleet age, fuel use, ridership, depot access and financing readiness; conduct distribution-grid screening; then compare a small set of investment scenarios before procurement.
