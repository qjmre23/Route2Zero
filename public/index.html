Method And Limits

  • Emissions: route kilometres times estimated weekday trips. This is activity volume, not measured tailpipe emissions.
  • Equity: population-weighted exposure to high-density 2020 WorldPop cells within a 300 m catchment.
  • Grid: 2024 Luzon renewable generation share for every route, not local charger or substation capacity.
  • Operator: neutral 50/100 placeholder until cooperative financing workshops provide route-level evidence.
  • AI boundary: optional text explains scores after ranking. No model output enters any score or rank.
