ROUTE2ZERO 2.0: COMPLETE SYSTEM IDEA

Route2Zero is a decision-support system for Metro Manila’s e-jeepney transition. It helps planners decide which corridors should be validated and prioritized first across climate benefit, equity, charging conditions, operator readiness, evidence quality, and uncertainty. The platform does not present one score as truth. It combines data, machine-learning estimates, deterministic impact models, sensitivity analysis, and constrained portfolio so decision-makers see why a corridor is promising, how stable that conclusion is, and what evidence could reverse it.

The system begins with 1,522 route-direction records from the historic Sakay GTFS baseline. Inputs remain immutable. A source registry records organization, period, geography, license, currentness, checksum, and limitations. A validation ledger records whether each corridor is historic-only, desk-checked, operator-confirmed, LGU-confirmed, or field-checked, preventing old schedules from being described as current operations. Geometry and city tags expose methods. Mapbox supplies a street-following planning line, labelled DERIVED, never official franchise geometry, while an approximate stop-sequence fallback remains visible when routing fails.

A feature store gives analytical modules consistent route identities and non-leaky inputs. The service-intensity model uses grouped cross-validation so opposite directions cannot cross training and validation folds. It predicts service intensity, never passenger demand or ridership. Historic or verified evidence remains primary; the model provides labelled estimates for missing or stale records and flags anomalies. Deterministic K-means typology groups corridor forms for filtering and interpretation, but clusters never add policy points or imply poverty or informal-settlement status.

The climate engine converts route length and service intensity into electrification scenarios. It calculates electric vehicle-kilometres, diesel displaced, electricity required, baseline emissions, grid emissions, and net carbon-dioxide-equivalent avoided. Factors are externalized and source-labelled; negative outcomes remain visible. Equity uses population exposure and accessibility signals, marking WorldPop results as area-level proxies rather than household poverty, vulnerability, or informal status. Charging readiness uses mapped substations, chargers, and terminals but never treats proximity as capacity proof. Operator readiness separates verified observations from a neutral prior instead of converting missing evidence into zero.

An evidence-confidence engine scores freshness, directness, specificity, completeness, validation, and reliability. Priority score, evidence grade, prediction uncertainty, and rank stability remain separate. Human users control climate, equity, charging, and operator weights. A scenario identifier records weights, filters, assumptions, and constraints. Reproducible Monte Carlo testing estimates top-ten probability, median rank, rank intervals, and robustness across thousands of policy scenarios. Value-of-information analysis perturbs unresolved fields to show which validation task could change rank or portfolio membership.

The Phase-1 optimizer converts a ranked list into a shortlist. It selects a number of corridors while enforcing constraints such as evidence grade, equity floor, city coverage, and inactive-route exclusions. Cost, fleet, or charging-capacity limits activate only with sourced scenario data; hypothetical inputs stay labelled. Results include selected corridors, binding constraints, climate ranges, evidence distribution, near selections, and exclusion reasons. Infeasible combinations return an explanation, never a fabricated answer.

The dashboard provides Overview, Corridor Map, Route Lens, Scenario Lab, Phase-1 Portfolio, Evidence and AI, and Method and Sources. Eight cards separate priority, evidence, climate, equity, charging, operator readiness, robustness, and typology. Map layers display dimensions, geometry status, terminals, and infrastructure context. Desktop and mobile layouts support keyboard navigation, controls, legends, scenario comparison, evidence queues, health, and PDF, Word, CSV, and JSON exports. Decision packs include build, source, model, scenario, assumptions, timestamps, caveats, and a warning that output does not authorize procurement, lending, franchise cancellation, or investment.

The Planning and Evidence Assistant receives precomputed facts. It can explain priority, compare scenarios, identify assumptions, and recommend evidence collection. It cannot edit scores, invent measurements, fetch arbitrary sources, expose secrets, or describe unverified evidence as verified. Requests carry scenario and build identifiers; schema validation, limits, timeouts, sanitization, and deterministic fallback preserve usefulness when the model is unavailable. Netlify serves static assets and serverless functions. MAPBOX_TOKEN may override the embedded public token, while the private AI credential remains server-side in ABSK_KEY with optional BASE_URL, MODEL, and AI_EXPLANATIONS_ENABLED settings.

The six-month pilot turns uncertainty into validation: establish source and model protocols; refresh routes and collect operator, charging, equity, and geometry evidence; validate estimates; calibrate climate and confidence rules; co-design weights and constraints; test comprehension and fairness; then deliver decision packs, model cards, source registries, policy briefs, automated tests, city adapters, refresh runbooks, and named owners. Success measures ground-check coverage, evidence-grade improvement, stakeholder validations, model error, decision stability, usability, and acceptance of one decision pack.
