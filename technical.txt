3. DECISION TERMINOLOGY

3.1 Priority score is the weighted result under a named human-controlled policy lens.
3.2 Evidence confidence describes the quality and specificity of supporting evidence.
3.3 Evidence grade maps evidence confidence to A, B, C, or D.
3.4 ML prediction error describes service-model performance against its target.
3.5 Rank stability describes behavior under tested policy-weight changes.
3.6 Top-10 probability is a frequency across simulations, not a truth probability.
3.7 P10-P90 rank is an empirical simulation range.
3.8 Climate low, base, and high values are scenario results.
3.9 Portfolio membership is the result of the current constraints.
3.10 Value of information is a deterministic perturbation priority.
3.11 Current validation means accepted date-stamped evidence under the pilot protocol.
3.12 Historic-only means no accepted current validation has been supplied.
3.13 Neutral prior means an explicit default due to insufficient evidence.
3.14 Proxy means indirect evidence with a known conceptual limitation.
3.15 Model-estimated means produced by a versioned fitted model.
3.16 No one term above substitutes for another.
3.17 A robust priority can still have weak evidence.
3.18 A high evidence grade can still have a scenario-dependent rank.
3.19 A model can have a strong historic metric and weak current validity.
