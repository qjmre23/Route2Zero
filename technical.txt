0007 | ==============================================================================
0008 | 1. PRODUCT CONTRACT
0009 | ==============================================================================
0010 | 1.01 Route2Zero ranks jeepney route-direction records for deeper electrification planning.
0011 | 1.02 The product is decision support and does not authorize funding, procurement, or deployment.
0012 | 1.03 The canonical route universe is the set of Sakay GTFS master route IDs containing PUJ.
0013 | 1.04 The current universe contains 1,522 jeepney route-direction records.
0014 | 1.05 A duplicated route_long_name may represent opposite GTFS directions and is not automatically removed.
0015 | 1.06 A companion corridor export groups repeated names while retaining direction_count.
0016 | 1.07 The dashboard exposes all four component scores before presenting the composite score.
0017 | 1.08 The default weights are emissions 35, equity 35, grid 15, and operator 15 percent.
0018 | 1.09 Live dashboard weights are normalized to sum to one before recomputing a score.
0019 | 1.10 AI-generated narratives are downstream of deterministic scores and ranking.
0020 | 1.11 No AI response can write to score columns or change rank order.
0021 | 1.12 Historic GTFS service dates span 2013 through 2020 and are labelled visibly.
0022 | 1.13 WorldPop is used as a population-density fallback rather than a settlement-status layer.
0023 | 1.14 Grid feasibility is a shared Luzon-level proxy rather than route-level charger capacity.
0024 | 1.15 Operator readiness defaults to a neutral 50 pending cooperative evidence.
0025 | 1.16 Missing values are preserved as missing or labelled placeholders; they are never silently fabricated.
0026 | 1.17 The MVP includes a city lens, but route-city tags require LGU validation.
0027 | 1.18 Every final route record retains source and confidence fields for each dimension.
0028 | 1.19 Policy briefs translate score outputs into evidence requests rather than investment recommendations.
0029 | 1.20 The six-month Pilot Plan is the route from screening output to city-owned validation.

0144 | 3. SOURCE DATA AND PROVENANCE
0145 | ==============================================================================
0146 | 3.01 The Sakay GTFS repository is https://github.com/sakayph/gtfs.
0147 | 3.02 The master branch is canonical because it is the repository default and has the newer service horizon.
0148 | 3.03 The dotc branch is retained only for comparison and reproducibility notes.
0149 | 3.04 The master feed contains 1,717 routes: 1,522 PUJ and 189 PUB records by exact route-ID classification.
0150 | 3.05 The dotc feed contains 1,715 routes and an extra route_bikes_allowed field.
0151 | 3.06 The audit found zero broken route-to-trip, trip-to-stop-time, or stop-time-to-stop references.
0152 | 3.07 The master service calendar extends through 2020 and therefore remains historic in 2026.
0153 | 3.08 The WorldPop source is the 2020 Philippines 1 km population-count raster.
0154 | 3.09 The WorldPop summary page is https://hub.worldpop.org/geodata/summary?id=33241.
0155 | 3.10 The WorldPop DOI recorded in methodology is 10.5258/SOTON/WP00670.
0156 | 3.11 The downloaded raster filename is phl_ppp_2020_1km_Aggregated.tif.
0157 | 3.12 The recorded raster SHA-256 begins DB87B and ends 9FD; see data_provenance.md for the full value.
0158 | 3.13 No retrievable corridor-resolution NAMRIA or PSA informal-settlement layer was used.
0159 | 3.14 WorldPop density exposure must not be interpreted as poverty, tenure, or informal-settlement status.
0160 | 3.15 The Philippine DOE 2024 Key Energy Statistics provides the Luzon generation values.
0161 | 3.16 The recorded values are 14,550 GWh renewable generation and 90,269 GWh total generation.
0162 | 3.17 The resulting shared renewable-share proxy is 16.118 percent before display rounding.
0163 | 3.18 The National Grid Emission Factor context is recorded as 0.7181 tCO2/MWh.
0164 | 3.19 The grid values are regional context and contain no feeder, depot, or charger-capacity information.
0165 | 3.20 Every external source URL is retained in docs/data_provenance.md and docs/methodology.md.
0166 | 3.21 Raw GTFS and reference files are never overwritten by pipeline stages.
0167 | 3.22 Processed outputs are regenerated from raw inputs and explicit constants.
0168 | 3.23 The presentation speaker notes include source blocks for every external claim and visual.
0169 | 3.24 Licensing and attribution remain the responsibility of downstream public release owners.

0691 | 7. SCORING AND CONFIDENCE RULES
0692 | ==============================================================================
0693 | 7.01 All component scores are represented on a zero-to-one-hundred scale.
0694 | 7.02 Emissions potential is min-max normalized from daily_vehicle_km_proxy.
0695 | 7.03 Equity is derived from WorldPop density exposure within a 300-metre route catchment.
0696 | 7.04 Grid feasibility is the shared 16.118-percent Luzon renewable-generation share.
0697 | 7.05 Operator readiness is 50 until a documented route override exists.
0698 | 7.06 The default composite is 0.35E + 0.35Q + 0.15G + 0.15O.
0699 | 7.07 Complete routes are ranked in descending composite order.
0700 | 7.08 One route lacks sufficient frequency and emissions evidence and remains unranked by default.
0701 | 7.09 The dashboard can include a low-confidence incomplete route only through an explicit opt-in.
0702 | 7.10 When incomplete routes are included, available weights are renormalized and a confidence penalty applies.
0703 | 7.11 The incomplete-route path is not needed by the current 1,521 complete records.
0704 | 7.12 Geometry confidence is highest for actual shapes and lower for stop-sequence approximations.
0705 | 7.13 Schedule confidence depends on available weekday trip observations.
0706 | 7.14 Equity confidence stays limited because the proxy is density-only and historic.
0707 | 7.15 Grid confidence stays limited because the value is regional and spatially constant.
0708 | 7.16 Operator confidence stays limited while the placeholder flag is true.
0709 | 7.17 Overall confidence summarizes the weakest relevant evidence boundaries.
0710 | 7.18 Ranking metadata always records ranking_ai_influence as false.
0711 | 7.19 AI narrative status is stored outside the score table in the explanation cache.
0712 | 7.20 No explanation text is parsed back into a numeric feature.
0713 | 7.21 Changing live weights does not mutate the persisted default score table.
0714 | 7.22 City filtering changes the route set but not the meaning of any score field.
0715 | 7.23 Route direction records remain separate to preserve GTFS identity.
0716 | 7.24 Corridor-level summaries are supporting views and do not replace the route-ID primary key.
0717 | 7.25 Any future local grid factor must carry source, spatial resolution, date, and confidence metadata.
0718 | 7.26 Any future operator override must carry reviewer, date, note, and evidence source.
0719 | 7.27 Any future equity layer must state whether it measures density, deprivation, tenure, access, or another concept.
0720 | 7.28 Any future emissions method must distinguish activity, energy use, tailpipe emissions, and life-cycle emissions.
0721 | 7.29 Sensitivity analysis should publish alternative weights rather than replacing the default silently.
0722 | 7.30 Public reporting should show both rank and underlying dimension values.
